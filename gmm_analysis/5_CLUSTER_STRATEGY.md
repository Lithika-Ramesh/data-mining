## FAQ

**Q: Can students move between clusters?**
A: Yes! Recalculate weekly. Goal is moving Cluster 1 → higher clusters.

**Q: Do students know their cluster?**
A: Typically no. Use behind-the-scenes for personalization without labeling.

**Q: What if a student fits multiple clusters?**
A: GMM provides probability scores. Use highest probability cluster, but note uncertainty.

**Q: How often to retrain the model?**
A: Initial model for cohort, then monitor. Retrain between cohorts with updated data.

**Q: Can we use this for predictions?**
A: Yes! Early cluster assignment predicts outcomes. Use for early intervention.
